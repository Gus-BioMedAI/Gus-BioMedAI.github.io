Multimodal survival modelling for lung cancer

Master's thesis, UPM. Placement at Hospital Clínico San Carlos, supervised by Laura Llorente-Sanz. Defended July 2026.

Does adding CT radiomics to clinical variables improve survival prediction in non-small-cell lung cancer, and would oncologists use the result? I built a late-fusion survival model on the public NSCLC-Radiomics cohort and linked it to the NLP pipeline below through a shared variable schema, so hospital notes and the public cohort use the same variables.

Fusion beat imaging-only (0.577) and clinical-only (0.539) on concordance. The gain is modest, and I report it together with calibration. In a Technology Acceptance Model survey of 8 respondents (5 oncologists, 3 biomedical engineers), clinicians rated usefulness and adoption intent between 4.4 and 4.8 out of 5.

C-index 0.587 Patients 421 Integrated Brier score 0.21

Spanish clinical NER for oncology notes

AI Research Intern, Hospital Clínico San Carlos, February to July 2026

Extracts ten oncology variables from free-text Spanish lung-cancer notes: histology, TNM, stage, ECOG, biomarkers, PD-L1, treatment and dates. The model is a frozen bsc-bio-ehr-es encoder with a trainable linear head (PyTorch, Hugging Face Transformers).

There was no labelled data, so I built the gold standard from scratch: regex and gazetteer pre-annotation, reviewed by hand in Doccano, with a blind intra-annotator reliability check. Evaluated with patient-level 5-fold cross-validation, so no patient appears in both training and test. Everything ran on-premise and offline under GDPR and LOPDGDD.

Micro-F1, overlap 0.71 Micro-F1, strict 0.47 Annotated spans 1,432 Notes / patients 108 / 43

InflammEye

Personal project in rheumatology and thermal imaging

Per-joint inflammation monitoring for rheumatoid arthritis from FLIR hand thermograms. MediaPipe locates the joints, a LightGBM model returns an inflammation probability for each one with a SHAP explanation, and a composite ThermoJIS score summarises the hand. It correlates with CDAI (ρ = 0.81) and SDAI (ρ = 0.83).

Built end to end: a FastAPI model service, and a React and TypeScript front end with Supabase auth and storage, designed with EU MDR and AEMPS in mind. A research prototype, not a diagnostic device. Watch the demo.

Patients 136 Joints 3,982 ROC-AUC 0.84 PR-AUC 0.33 at 7.1% prevalence
