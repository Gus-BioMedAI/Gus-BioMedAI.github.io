Who I Am

I'm Gustavo, a 24-year-old biomedical engineer currently finishing my final semester at UPM in Madrid — having completed my bachelor's at Politecnico di Milano. Two of Europe's most demanding engineering schools, two cities, one thread.

At my core, I'm a systems thinker. I find meaning in understanding how parts connect to form something larger — whether in the human body, a clinical workflow, or a research pipeline.
